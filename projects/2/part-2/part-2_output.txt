(c) What x_train[0] represents:
  A single review as a 200-dimensional multi-hot vector: position i is 1.0
  if word index i occurs anywhere in the review, else 0.0. Order and
  frequency are discarded (bag of words).
  Measured on the first training review: 68 of 200 positions set.
  Indices 0-2 are reserved markers (padding, sequence start, out-of-vocabulary),
  so the count includes the start and OOV markers, not only real words.
